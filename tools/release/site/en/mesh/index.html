Device mesh

Your phone, computer and server – one team. Start on one device and carry on with another.

Continue where you stopped

What is open on one device moves to another paired one. Nothing arrives unasked: the target device always asks you, and with several possible devices the sender asks which one. What you do not accept within a minute is declined.

What your devices can do together

Continue on another device

From the app menu or with Super+M, or from the mesh tile in quick settings. A file opens at the same place.

Send files

Send a file to a paired device; the other device asks before it stores anything.

Shared clipboard

Copy here, paste there (text up to 16 KB). Confidential entries – such as passwords – never leave the device. New in development.

Lending the AI model

The phone thinks with the computer's model and acts on itself; question and answer stay inside the encrypted channel.

The helper acts on another device

Ask your phone's helper to open an app on your computer – only if the computer granted the right “Control this device”, and the person there confirms anything dangerous.

Devices in different networks

Through our server's relay: it forwards ciphertext only, and the encrypted channel stays end to end.

Secure from the first pairing

  • Pairing with a six-digit code over SPAKE2, bound to both devices' keys: at most five attempts and five minutes.
  • Every connection in a Noise channel (X25519, ChaCha20-Poly1305) in which both sides prove their keys.
  • Rights per device: a newly paired device may only offer activities, files and the clipboard; seeing the screen, control, sound, computing and the AI model stay off until you grant them.
  • An off switch on every device, down to the kernel. The mesh service runs as its own user without any kernel right.

Honest status

Works in the current release

  • The service, the “Mesh” page in Settings with pairing and rights, the quick-settings tile, “Continue on another device”, and the question on the target device
  • Tested against our real server with injected faults (drops, damage, delays, crashes)

New in development

  • The shared clipboard between paired devices

Not there yet

  • Trying it on real devices, and the “hand over” gesture on the phone
  • Remote view and control, sound, calls and notifications between devices
  • Sending and receiving files in the installed system (tested in the service only)
  • TVs and speakers as mesh devices
