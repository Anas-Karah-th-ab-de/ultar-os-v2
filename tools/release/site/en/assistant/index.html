The helper

A helper that runs on your own device – it understands your words and acts through the system, and does nothing dangerous without your consent.

Where the helper thinks

The helper picks the best “brain” available by itself, in this order. How a model computes (processor, graphics chip or neural chip) is the model service's matter; the helper only sees whether it is ready and how big it is.

  1. A model on your deviceThe best choice: nothing leaves the device.
  2. A model of a paired deviceIt thinks there and acts here – through the encrypted mesh.
  3. A cloud model with your keyOnly as the last way, with your own key at the provider (for example Claude).
  4. The smallest helpFixed commands and answers about the system in Arabic, English and German – without a model.

What it can do

Apps and device

Open apps, volume and mute, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and flight mode, screenshots, lock and sleep.

Settings

Reads and changes settings – sensitive areas after your confirmation, your own keys and admin keys never.

The mesh

“Continue this on the computer”, “send the file to the phone” – and the other device asks.

Knowledge

Explains pairing, continuing, files, the model, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and updates – even without a model.

Always under your control

  • Off until you switch it on in Settings → Assistant; the model service runs only then.
  • It acts only when asked, and only with fixed tools; a tool a model invents is refused and never run.
  • Restart, power-off and anything dangerous goes through the system dialog and needs your confirmation.
  • Its own sheet (Super+H) shows every step with a “Stop” button, and the page “What the helper may do” has a log of everything it did.
  • It runs as its own user with narrow rights, through the system bus only – without administrator rights.

Honest status

Works in the current release

  • The service, the helper sheet and the rights page are built and tested, together with the mesh as two devices in the test

Not there yet

  • Voice control “Hey KM”, speech recognition and speech output
  • Running models on the graphics and neural chips through the Ultar kernel
  • Trying it on real hardware and with a real model in the installed system
