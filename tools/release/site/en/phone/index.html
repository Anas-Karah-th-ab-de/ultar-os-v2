Ultar Phone

The phone's own interface, separate from the desktop, on the same secure base.

Made for touch

Lock screen

A dot-matrix clock and the Hijri date. Unlocking always goes through the sign-in service.

Home screen

App pages, a dock and the cards “Next” and “Device”.

Quick settings

Wi-Fi, mobile data and more with one touch.

Recent apps and search

Switch between apps and search everything.

Calls over the Ultar number

Voice and video, end-to-end encrypted.

A phone that becomes a computer

The goal: connect the phone to a screen and keyboard and the desktop appears.

Honest status

Works in the current release

  • The phone interface is tested automatically in Arabic, English and German, light and dark, every button pressed
  • USB image for x86_64 devices (x86 phones and tablets, QEMU) – tested in QEMU

Not there yet

  • The Samsung Galaxy A30 package has not been started on a real phone yet, and a phone without an account opens without a lock
  • The Ultar kernel for ARM64 is not finished; the A30 package runs on Samsung's own Linux kernel
  • Cellular calls, SMS and USSD
