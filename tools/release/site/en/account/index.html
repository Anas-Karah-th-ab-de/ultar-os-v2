Ultar account

The Ultar account is optional: the device works fully without it.

What the account gives you

Ultar number

A number like UOS-XXXX-XXXX for end-to-end encrypted voice and video calls.

Mesh over the internet

Your devices find each other even in different networks, through our server's relay.

Your devices

Every device has its own key; a new device needs your approval, and recovery comes with a waiting time and a warning on all your devices.

The store and system updates need no account.

How to create an account

  1. Open Settings

    Settings → Accounts → Ultar account.

  2. Create the account or sign in

    User name and password. The device key is made on your device and never leaves it.

  3. Switch on what you want

    Receiving Ultar calls and the mesh over the internet – each service has its own switch, and receiving calls is off from the start.

Today you manage the account and devices in the device's Settings. Managing them on the web is planned for later.
